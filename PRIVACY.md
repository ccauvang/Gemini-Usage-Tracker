# Privacy Policy: Gemini Usage Tracker

Last updated: October 22, 2026

Gemini Usage Tracker shows your Gemini usage limits (session and weekly) on gemini.google.com.

## Data we collect

None. The extension does not collect, store on any server, sell, share or transmit your data.

## What it does on your device

- Reads your usage numbers (percent, plan name, reset time) from your own Gemini usage page, only inside your browser.
- Saves your "show chat bar ring" setting and the last usage numbers locally with chrome.storage.local. This stays on your device.

## Third parties

No analytics, no ads, no tracking, no remote servers. The extension does not talk to any website other than gemini.google.com.

## Permissions

- declarativeNetRequest: lets the hidden usage page load inside Gemini.
- storage: saves your setting and last numbers locally.
- gemini.google.com access: show the panel and read your usage page.
